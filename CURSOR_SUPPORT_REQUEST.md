# Cursor Support Request Template

**Purpose:** Request data deletion and clarification about data handling  
**To:** Cursor Support  
**Subject:** Data Deletion Request and Privacy Policy Clarification

---

## Message Template

Dear Cursor Support Team,

I am writing to request information and actions regarding my account's conversation data and privacy settings.

### Request 1: Deletion of Past Conversation History

I would like to request the deletion of all past conversation history associated with my account. During recent work, I inadvertently processed sensitive documents (including personally identifiable information, financial records, and compliance materials) through conversations with the AI assistant, and I was not aware that Privacy Mode was available or should be enabled.

**Specific Date/Time Range for Deletion Request:**
- Start Date/Time: December 27, 2025 at approximately 7:36 AM
- End Date/Time: December 28, 2025 at approximately 8:17 PM

Please delete all conversation history within this timeframe. If conversation history cannot be deleted by specific date ranges, please delete all conversation history for my account from the beginning of December 27, 2025 through the end of December 28, 2025.

Please confirm:
- Whether conversation history deletion is possible for my account
- The process for requesting such deletion
- Any limitations on what can be deleted (e.g., if data was already used in training)
- The timeline for completion of any deletion request

### Request 2: Clarification on Data Usage for Training

I was not aware of Privacy Mode during my conversations and did not have it enabled. I need clarification on:

1. Whether my conversation data (from conversations where Privacy Mode was NOT enabled) was used for:
   - Model training
   - Model improvement
   - Service enhancement
   - Any other purposes

2. What the default data handling policy is for accounts without Privacy Mode enabled

3. Whether there are any agreements with third-party AI providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic) that prevent my data from being used in training, even without Privacy Mode enabled

### Request 3: Data Handling Confirmation

Please provide confirmation regarding:

1. **Data Retention:**
   - How long conversation logs are retained for accounts without Privacy Mode
   - Whether there are different retention periods for different types of data
   - When (if ever) conversation data is permanently deleted

2. **Data Access:**
   - Who has access to stored conversation data (Cursor staff, third-party providers, etc.)
   - What security measures are in place to protect stored data
   - Whether data is encrypted at rest and in transit

3. **Third-Party Sharing:**
   - Whether conversation data is shared with third-party AI providers
   - What agreements or controls are in place regarding third-party data usage
   - Whether data is anonymized before sharing

4. **Account-Specific Information:**
   - Confirmation of the current Privacy Mode status for my account
   - Any other privacy settings that should be reviewed
   - Steps I should take to maximize privacy protection going forward

### Context

I work in an educational/accreditation context and inadvertently processed sensitive institutional documents including:
- Personal information (resumes, credentials)
- Financial statements and budgets
- Compliance and accreditation materials
- Student and personnel records policies

I need to understand the full scope of data exposure to assess potential compliance requirements and notification obligations.

### Response Requested

Please provide a comprehensive response addressing all three requests above. If written documentation (privacy policy, terms of service) addresses any of these questions, please provide relevant links or sections.

I understand that some information may be covered by your privacy policy, but I would appreciate specific, direct answers to these questions given the sensitive nature of the data involved.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Email]
[Your Account Email/Username]

## Expected Response Time

Most support teams respond within:
- 24-48 hours for urgent matters
- 3-5 business days for standard requests
- Longer for complex privacy/data requests

If you don't receive a response within a week, consider:
- Following up with a reminder
- Escalating if escalation options are available
- Consulting with legal counsel about next steps
